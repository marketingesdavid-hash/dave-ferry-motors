Grant Lane: a life packed onto a Harley

We met Grant Lane from Fort Worth, Texas, with his 2018 Harley-Davidson Fat Bob. At the time of our conversation, he described covering around 25,000 miles in seven months. His 114ci engine and a roughly two-inch rear lift are part of a setup shaped by long days in the saddle.

Across these short conversations, Grant shows the man behind the motorcycle, the meaning of his vest patches and the equipment he carries for solo travel. The Big Bend setting gives the practical details their context: fuel, cooking gear and preparation matter when the next stop is far away.

Mineral Wells, Texas · Collector interview

The Mustang Baron: inside Finley Ledbetter’s world

At the Blue Oval Car Barn in Mineral Wells, Finley Ledbetter opens the doors to a world built around his passion for 1971 Ford Mustangs. Our conversation follows the cars into the workshop, exploring restorations, rare details and the personal story behind the collection.

The full interview gives the machines room to breathe, with workshop moments and classic pinball machines adding to the atmosphere. The shorter films below take a closer look at the collection and a 1971 Cobra Jet.
